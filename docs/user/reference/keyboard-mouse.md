# Keyboard and mouse

## Shortcuts (Trajectory Log Tools)

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| ++ctrl+o++ | Open files |
| ++ctrl+e++ | Export the ticked logs, or the shown log |
| ++ctrl+w++ | Close the ticked logs, or the shown log (nothing is deleted) |
| ++f1++ | This guide, at the page for the window you're in (both apps) |
| ++enter++ in **Find** (Raw tab) | Find next |

## Scrolling: one thing scrolls at a time

Wherever the mouse is, the wheel scrolls exactly one thing:

- **On a page** (log, Compare, Rebuild) and in the Export and Header details windows, tables and texts
  are shown at full length with no scroll bar of their own, so the wheel scrolls the page whether the
  mouse is over a table or not.
- **In the File comparison window** and in Trajectory Log Converter's list, the tables fill the window
  and nothing around them scrolls, so the wheel scrolls the table under the mouse. Drag the bar between
  File comparison's two tables to share the room.
- ++shift++ + wheel scrolls a table that is wider than its space sideways.

## Charts

| Where | Zoom | Pan | Full view |
|---|---|---|---|
| On a page (log, Compare) | ++ctrl++ + wheel (the page doesn't move) | Drag | Double-click |
| In a window of its own (Rebuild details, or right-click > **Open in New Window**) | Wheel | Drag | Double-click |

On the **Axes** tab, zooming either chart zooms both; on the **MLC** tab, the map and the RMS chart
share the leaf axis.

## Time pickers

Select a time to choose the hour and minute from a list, then **Done**. Or, on the time, turn the wheel
or press ++up++ / ++down++ to change it a minute at a time (with ++shift++, an hour).

## Drag and drop

- Drop `.bin` logs or folders anywhere on the Trajectory Log Tools window to open them.
- On the Compare page, drop a log onto **A** or **B** to replace it.
- On the Rebuild page, drop Eclipse export `.csv` files to add them.
