# Tutorials

Lessons for your first time with the app. Follow the steps in order; each takes about ten minutes.

- [Your first look at a log](first-look.md): open a log, read its key facts, and explore the
  timeline, the axes and the MLC.
- [Your first delivery comparison](first-comparison.md): compare two fractions of a plan and read
  the result.

The pictures show synthetic demo logs (patients `PHANTOM-01` to `PHANTOM-05`), never patient data.
Use any log you're allowed to open; what you see will differ in the details.
